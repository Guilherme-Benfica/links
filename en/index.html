Frequently asked questions

Are the practice exams available in Portuguese?

It depends on the course. CLF-C02, AIF-C01, AIB-C01, SAA-C03, and MLA-C02 come in English and Portuguese, with the same questions and explanations: just pick PT or EN on the card. SOA-C03, DEA-C01, and AIP-C01 are available in English.

Which practice exam should I take first?

Start from your goal: general cloud (CLF-C02), AI without coding (AIF-C01 and AIB-C01), architecture (SAA-C03), operations (SOA-C03), data (DEA-C01), machine learning (MLA-C02), or generative AI for developers (AIP-C01). If you are new to AWS, CLF-C02 is a good first step.

Are these the real exam questions?

No. They are original questions written from each official exam guide, to train the same kind of reasoning the exam tests.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not for CLF-C02, AIF-C01, or AIB-C01. The others are Associate or Professional level and expect hands-on AWS experience. DEA-C01 expects familiarity with data pipelines, and MLA-C02 and AIP-C01 expect familiarity with machine learning and AI development.

Does the card link already apply the coupon?

Yes. Each card link already carries the promotional coupon. Udemy coupons have an expiry date and a limited number of uses: if the full price shows up, the coupon has expired.

How long do I have access?

On Udemy, course access is lifetime and includes updates to the practice exams.
