Frequently asked questions

Are the practice exams available in Portuguese?

Yes. Each course has an English and a Portuguese version, with the same questions and explanations. Just pick PT or EN on the course card.

Are these the real exam questions?

No. They are original questions written from each official exam guide, to train the same kind of reasoning the exam tests.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not for CLF-C02, AIF-C01, or AIB-C01. MLA-C02 is an Associate-level exam and expects familiarity with data and machine learning on AWS.

How long do I have access?

On Udemy, course access is lifetime and includes updates to the practice exams.
